# Checklist starters

These are starting points only. Requirements differ by country, university and employer, and they change. Always tell the user to compare the list with the official requirements for their application, edit it, and confirm the final list before the search starts.

## Student visa

1. Passport (valid for the whole stay)
2. University offer or acceptance letter
3. Degree certificate
4. Academic transcripts
5. English test result, or a letter explaining an exemption
6. Bank statements or sponsor letter
7. Passport-size photo
8. Tuberculosis or medical test certificate, if required by the destination country

## University admission or scholarship

1. Passport or national ID
2. Degree certificate or provisional certificate
3. Academic transcripts
4. CV
5. Statement of purpose or study plan
6. Recommendation letters
7. Language test result or exemption letter
8. Passport-size photo

## Job onboarding

1. National ID or passport
2. Degree certificate
3. Experience or reference letters
4. CV
5. Bank account details letter or cheque copy
6. Tax registration document, if required
7. Passport-size photo

## Family travel

1. Passport for each traveller
2. National ID or birth certificate for each child
3. Travel insurance
4. Hotel or invitation letter
5. Return ticket booking

For family travel, run the Skill once per traveller so that name checks compare documents for one person at a time.
