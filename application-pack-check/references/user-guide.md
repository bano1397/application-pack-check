# Application Pack Check

Applying for a visa, a university place, a scholarship or a new job means collecting the same papers again and again: passport, degree, transcripts, bank statements, letters. They are spread across folders, old scans and phone photos, and a single problem (an expired passport, a missing transcript, or a name spelled differently on the degree) can get an application delayed or rejected.

Application Pack Check finds those papers on your UGREEN NAS, reads them, and tells you what is ready before you apply.

## What you get

- A readiness report for your checklist: Ready, Expiring, Expired, Name mismatch, Unclear or Missing for every item.
- A name and date-of-birth check across all your documents, so spelling differences are caught early.
- After you confirm, a new pack folder with clean, numbered copies (01-passport.pdf, 02-offer-letter.pdf and so on), plus the report saved inside it.

Your original files are never moved, renamed or deleted. Nothing is uploaded, emailed or shared. Document numbers are shown only by their last four characters.

## How to use it

Say something like:

- "Are my documents ready for my UK student visa?"
- "Prepare my scholarship application pack."
- "Check my papers before I apply for this job."

Uliya will ask you for:

1. The application name.
2. Your full name exactly as it is on your passport or ID.
3. The date you plan to submit.
4. Your checklist. You can start from a ready-made list and edit it.
5. Which folders to search, and whether to include photos of documents.
6. Where to create the pack folder.

You then see the report, choose which file to use for each item, and approve the copy.

## Good to know

- One applicant per run. For a family, run it once per person.
- The checklist starters are only a starting point. Always check the official requirements for your application.
- This is a checking aid, not legal or immigration advice.
